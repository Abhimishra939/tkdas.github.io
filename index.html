Academic profile

Research rooted in the field.
Ideas built for the future.

Advancing sustainable crop production through weed science, conservation agriculture and precision-based approaches.

Dr. T.K. Das is Principal Scientist and Professor in the Division of Agronomy at ICAR–Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI), New Delhi. His work focuses on weed science, conservation agriculture and precision agronomy for major cropping systems of India.

He has led long-term field experiments on residue management, tillage, herbicides and integrated weed management, contributing to resilient production systems, resource conservation and sustainable agricultural practices.

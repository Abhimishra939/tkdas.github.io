About

Dr. T.K. Das is a Principal Scientist in the Division of Agronomy at ICAR-Indian Agricultural Research Institute, New Delhi.

His research focuses on Weed Science, Conservation Agriculture, Herbicide Management, Sustainable Crop Production, and Precision Agronomy.

He has guided multiple M.Sc. and Ph.D. students and contributed extensively to agricultural research and education.

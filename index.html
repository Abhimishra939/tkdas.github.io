Academic profile

Research rooted in the field.
Ideas built for the future.

Advancing sustainable crop production through weed science, conservation agriculture and precision-based approaches.

Dr. T.K. Das is a Principal Scientist in the Division of Agronomy at ICAR–Indian Agricultural Research Institute, New Delhi.

His research focuses on weed science, conservation agriculture, herbicide management, sustainable crop production and precision agronomy—connecting scientific research with practical field-level solutions.

His work contributes to resilient production systems, resource conservation and the development of more sustainable agricultural practices.

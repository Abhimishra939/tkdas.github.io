About

Dr. T.K. Das is a Principal Scientist in the Division of Agronomy at ICAR-Indian Agricultural Research Institute, New Delhi.

His research centres on weed science, conservation agriculture, herbicide management, sustainable crop production and precision agronomy — work that shapes practical, field-level recommendations for Indian agriculture.

He has guided multiple M.Sc. and Ph.D. students and contributed extensively to agricultural research, mentorship and academic publishing.

Academic profile

Research rooted in the field.
Science built for global food security.

Over three decades of teaching, curriculum innovation, and long-term agronomic experimentation at ICAR–IARI, New Delhi and international universities.

Dr. Tapas Kumar Das serves as Professor (UG & PG) and Principal Scientist in the Division of Agronomy at ICAR–Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI), New Delhi. He earned his M.Sc. (3.97/4.00) and Ph.D. (3.82/4.00) in Agronomy from IARI and secured 4th All-India Rank in ICAR-ARS.

He has led 15+ year multi-cropping Conservation Agriculture trials across the Indo-Gangetic Plains, resolved national Phalaris minor herbicide cross-resistance challenges across 763 biotypes, and authored landmark textbooks including Weed Science: Basics and Applications (reprinted 9 times).

Core Scientific Contributions

  • Conservation Agriculture: Triple & double zero-till residue systems across 5 irrigated & 12 rainfed rotations
  • Herbicide Resistance: National management strategies for isoproturon & clodinafop-resistant Phalaris minor
  • Novel Concepts: Seed dormancy breakers (KNO3, GA3), missing-row wheat sowing & ETL regression models
  • Digital Agronomy Tools: Co-developer of WeedExpert, AgriGyaan, Herbicide Calculator & CRAN R-package WeedIndices
  • Global Teaching: ~31 years teaching across IARI (India), Alemaya University (Ethiopia) & ANASTU (Afghanistan)

# Dr. Tapas Kumar Das — Academic Portfolio Website

Official academic website for **Dr. Tapas Kumar Das** (*FNASc, FNAAS, FISA, FISWS, FSPPS*), Professor & Principal Scientist, Division of Agronomy, ICAR–Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI), New Delhi.

## Structure
- `index.html` — Home page with interactive particle canvas, 4 live themes (*Emerald Agron*, *Academic Royal*, *Midnight Dark*, *Terracotta Harvest*), Google Scholar portrait & metrics, Books/Chapters showcase, and Honours.
- `about.html` — Higher education, career timeline, ~31 years of teaching across India, Ethiopia, Afghanistan & Myanmar, and searchable list of 36 Chairman PG scholars (134+ total guided).
- `research.html` — Scientific concepts, field technologies, digital decision tools, major funded projects, and extension outreach.
- `achievements.html` — National Academy fellowships, ICAR Bharat Ratna Dr. C. Subramaniam Award, IARI Hooker & Best Teacher Awards, ISWS Presidency, and editorial service.
- `books.html` — Searchable & filterable showcase of all **12 Books/Manuals**, **89 Book Chapters**, and **4 E-Publications**.
- `publications.html` — Interactive bibliography covering **309+ Research Articles**, **22 Reviews**, **89 Book Chapters**, **88 Bulletins/Popular Articles**, and live Google Scholar records.
- `top50.html` — Curated Top 50 articles ranked by NAAS score.
- `contact.html` — Official institutional address and academic profile links.
- `assets/biodata_archive.js` — Sanitized structured academic dataset compiled from CV & Publication Annexures.
- `.github/workflows/update-scholar.yml` & `scripts/update_scholar.py` — Daily automated Google Scholar snapshot updater.

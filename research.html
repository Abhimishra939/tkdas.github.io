Scientific Breakthroughs

Novel Concepts & Field Technologies Developed

◈

Conservation Agriculture Systems

15+ year long-term trials across 5 irrigated (rice–wheat–mungbean, cotton–wheat, maize–wheat–mungbean, pigeonpea–wheat, rice–mustard) and 12 rainfed rotations; 15–35% higher water/energy productivity & SOC sequestration.

✳

Phalaris minor Resistance Solution

Screened 763 biotypes from ~760 villages of Punjab & Haryana; quantified cross-resistance indices and established national recommendations for clodinafop, sulfosulfuron, pinoxaden & herbicide rotations.

⌁

Chemical Seed Dormancy Breakers

First-in-world integration of KNO3 (6%) and GA3 (400 ppm) prior to pre-emergence herbicides for synchronized weed seedbank exhaustion, boosting soybean yield by 146%.

⌘

Missing-Row Wheat Sowing

Leaving 1 unsown drill row after every 4 sown rows increases wheat grain yield by 8–17%, reduces lodging, and saves 10–12% seed rate (~3.1 lakh tonnes national potential).

♧

ETL Regression & Bio-Efficacy Models

Introduced quadratic regression Economic Threshold Level (ETL) models for Cyperus rotundus, Trianthema portulacastrum, Chenopodium album & Phalaris minor, plus F–D ratio bio-efficacy metrics.

◎

Digital Apps & R-Package Tools

Co-developed Android apps (WeedExpert, AgriGyaan, Herbicide Calculator), web-based ISHUFC, and CRAN R-package WeedIndices computing 11 weed indices simultaneously.
